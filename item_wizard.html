Step 3 of 5

Choose its sprites

The bag icon and the sprite worn or held by the character are separate art. Use a sprite from the game, or draw your own in the sprite editor (32 × 32 pixels).

Inventory icon

On the character

Jewellery does not have a worn sprite. Your inventory icon is used in the bag.

Colour changes preview immediately on the chosen sprite. Pick “Colourize” to tint white or grey art. Browser previews approximate the game shader; original shading is preserved.

Step 4 of 5

Set its stats and bonuses

Keep the starting stats, or propose your own. Final numbers and availability are reviewed by the developer.

Rarity changes the proposed label; it does not rescale the starting stats.

Guaranteed built-in bonuses

Every copy of your proposed item carries the bonuses selected here. An effect such as “on block” still needs that event to happen. Any extra bonuses rolled when loot drops are separate.

New to item stats?

Strength supports health and melee damage. Dexterity supports critical hits and speed. Intelligence supports spells. A critical hit deals extra damage; haste speeds up actions, regeneration restores health, and resistance reduces damage from an element.

Drawbacks

A powerful unique can carry a cost to balance it, such as less health or more damage taken. Drawbacks are guaranteed like bonuses and show in red.

Random affix pool

When the item drops it also rolls random affixes, picked by these weights. Raise a weight to make that affix more likely; leave it blank to exclude it.
