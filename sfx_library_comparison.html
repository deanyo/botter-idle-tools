How this comparison was made

Actual combat was rendered once with Godot Movie Maker. The shared GameFeel source recorded actor impacts, casts, kills and pickups by video frame. Each ten-second section uses one school and a matching elemental weapon, with legal crit affixes. Each soundtrack was scheduled with Botter’s actual GDScript voice policy: the same pool, priorities, shared hit/crit throttles, pitch ladders and seeded per-event jitter. Streak presentation is recalculated from video presentation time, since offline rendering is not a wall-clock performance test.

Third-party impacts retain up to 750 ms of body/tail, with onset trim and a 25 ms fade. Maximum 50 ms RMS and peak are capped to the generated enemy-hit sample. Crits use the same source at the shipped +6 dB emphasis. All five use world volume 80%. No master normalization, compression or music. Offline linear resampling is deterministic; these are rescorings of real gameplay, not byte-identical audio-driver captures or a performance benchmark.

The original/prepared players below are private audition aids. Willosuna’s files may be used in games and video but may not be redistributed as a sound collection or registered with Content ID. No third-party audio from this test is committed to Botter or selected as the new default.
